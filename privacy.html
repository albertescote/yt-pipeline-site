Privacy Policy

yt-pipeline uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Data accessed

The only Google account that authorises yt-pipeline is the channel owner's own account. With that permission the tool:

  • uploads videos, metadata and thumbnails to that channel;
  • reads the titles of that channel's recent uploads, to avoid duplicates.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth refresh token is stored only on the owner's private server. Video titles are kept in a local publishing log. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics. No data from other YouTube users is collected.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings. Stored data is deleted within 7 days of revocation.

Contact

Through the contact email listed on the channel's YouTube "About" page.
