yt-pipeline

yt-pipeline is a private publishing tool used by a single creator to upload educational personal-finance videos to their own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to the creator's own channel using the YouTube Data API.
  • Checks the channel's recent uploads to avoid publishing the same video twice.

It is not offered to the public, has no user accounts and does not access anyone else's YouTube or Google data.
